# Home page brief (`/`, `/ja`) — 2026-10-15

Mode: Persuade, in the register of a bench rather than a pitch. The visitor acts once; the instrument finishes the experiment; what comes back is on the same screen.

## Ground (felt situation)

Every audience arrives not knowing what the instrument is, and most arrive sceptical of being sold to: a scientist at 23:00 from a signature link, an introducer checking what to relay, a phone in a Demo Day room, a person with no vocabulary. The common cognitive operation is model building: learn how a system behaves by acting on it. The domain's own ground-tone is a dark culture room, one light at a time, patience, and the one thing that happens when something is added. Discovery held inside caution.

## Map (experiential qualities)

Atmosphere: the card's room, unchanged. Invitation: the first change goes in as you arrive, so the gesture is shown before it is asked. Resonance: a plate, wells, a control row, a dose series, a curve with its points; anyone who runs assays recognises their world without a label, anyone else reads it by physics. Tension and resolution: press (commitment) → the bead falls → cells light over seconds (unfolding) → the point rises in the record and the instrument answers with the next change (consummation). Presence: the watcher looks where the pointer is; the instrument follows what you did.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is a bench where the visitor does one experiment and the instrument finishes it. It refuses the film, the scroll tour, the feature list, the caption that carries structure, and any sentence that concludes for the reader.

OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md. One material: light per unit time. The plate is known by what it hides; a change is a bead of liquid; an answer is a cell's own colour; the record is points of light and one curve of trajectory light, no axes, no ticks. Text lives in the light's column. Nothing drawn as an outline anywhere.

STORY: the visitor understands that a change goes into one well of living cells while the plate stays put, that the cells answer in place over seconds beside wells never touched, that the next change follows from the answer, and that what comes back is a model with its evidence. They believe it because they did it. They do one of: write, relay the sentence, or read on to the people and the stage.

FIRST VIEWPORT (of the bench, 1440×900): right 7/12 the plate from over it, 3×3 whole wells and the ring cut by the fade, fixed view; left 5/12 the heading in two halves (≤ 16 words), beneath it the record (points and curve, ~320×200), beneath that one line counting the changes and whose. Primary action: press and hold a well. Affordances: the arrival change, the watcher's light following the pointer, a crosshair over a well. At 390: heading, plate (~55svh), record, count; all in one screen.

FORM: the bench, first of five structures derived (bench; one well close; the run; two plates; the watcher's view). concept-seed was not run; the choice was put to the user through the question tool and locked.

Interaction grammar (binding): hover = look, press = act, hold = stronger, release = the drop falls; the same on touch and mouse; keyboard: arrows choose a well, Space/Enter held = press. A passing pointer changes nothing. The instrument acts on its own only in answer to an answer (its own or the visitor's), never on a cue the visitor must wait for; the one exception is the arrival change. The view never moves. No captions over the plate. One sentence per section names what the light has shown.

Decisions to judge at review: the instrument continuing its own row when the visitor is idle (so a passive phone sees the loop close); the record beside the plate at small scale and again large in the readout.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
