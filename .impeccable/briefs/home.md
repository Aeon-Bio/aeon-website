# Home page brief (`/`, `/ja`) — 2026-10-15

Mode: Persuade, in the register of a bench rather than a pitch. The instrument does the experiment in the open; a visitor may add one question with a tap; what comes back is on the same screen.

Revised 2026-10-16 after the user's verdict on the first build ("too obtuse; we can't demand too much from expecting people to interact with this in super-specific ways"; the fitted curve "does nothing, especially reused"): the gesture grammar and the curve are gone.

## Ground (felt situation)

Every audience arrives not knowing what the instrument is, and most arrive sceptical of being sold to: a scientist at 23:00 from a signature link, an introducer checking what to relay, a phone in a Demo Day room, a person with no vocabulary. The common cognitive operation is model building: learn how a system behaves by acting on it. The domain's own ground-tone is a dark culture room, one light at a time, patience, and the one thing that happens when something is added. Discovery held inside caution.

## Map (experiential qualities)

Atmosphere: the card's room, unchanged. Invitation: the first change goes in as you arrive, and the instrument carries on; nothing is asked. Resonance: a plate, wells, a control row, a dose series read as the wells themselves with their beads; anyone who runs assays recognises their world without a label, anyone else reads it by physics. Tension and resolution: a bead falls (commitment) → cells light over seconds (unfolding) → the next bead is sized from that answer (consummation), three times over in the first half minute. Presence: the instrument follows what you did, if you did anything.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the page is a bench where the instrument does one experiment in the open and a visitor may add a question to it. It refuses the film, the scroll tour, the feature list, the caption that carries structure, and any sentence that concludes for the reader.

OWN-WORLD: DESIGN.md. One material: light per unit time. The plate is known by what it hides; a change is a bead of liquid; an answer is a cell's own colour; the record is the changed wells drawn as themselves with the bead that went into each, no axes, no ticks, no curve. Text lives in the light's column. Nothing drawn as an outline anywhere.

STORY: the visitor understands that a change goes into one well of living cells while the plate stays put, that the cells answer in place over seconds beside wells never touched, that the next change follows from the answer, and that what comes back is a model with its evidence. They believe it because they watched it happen, and could add to it. They do one of: write, relay the sentence, or read on to the people and the stage.

FIRST VIEWPORT (of the bench, 1440×900): right 7/12 the plate from over it, 3×3 whole wells and the ring cut by the fade, fixed view; left 5/12 the heading in two halves (≤ 16 words), beneath it the record (the changed wells in a strip, ~384×128), beneath that one line counting the changes and whose. Primary action: none required; a tap on a well adds a change. Affordances: the arrival change and the instrument carrying on; the pointer cursor over the plate. At 390: heading, plate (~55svh), record, count; all in one screen.

FORM: the bench, first of five structures derived (bench; one well close; the run; two plates; the watcher's view). concept-seed was not run; the choice was put to the user through the question tool and locked.

Interaction grammar (binding): tap or click = one change in, at the one strength every change starts at. Nothing else. No hover state, no hold, no keyboard grammar beyond a button that does the same. The instrument acts on its own on arrival and after every settled answer, so a reader who never touches the plate sees the whole chain. The view never moves. No captions over the plate. One sentence per section names what the light has shown.

Decisions to judge at review: the search gain (a row of three reads weak, too strong, between); the record beside the plate at small scale and again large in the readout.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
